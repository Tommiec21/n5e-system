# CARDS1: class cards from the book's boxes

King 2026-11-25: "a bunch of Condition, Amp and Summon Cards that you need screenshots of to resolve/Fix. Give me that list. Lets focus on stuff from Classes first."

Status: **classes done (0.112.0-alpha.1).** Clans next when King asks.

## Finding

- Orochimaru's Observation Compendium has no rules cards as pictures (its 190 images are art). Every card is a Word text box; all 52 class card boxes were checked against the system: their text was all imported.
- Four cards were in the wrong place; King sent the boxes (screenshots) and they were fixed:

## Fixed

1. **Transfiguration** (Medical-Nin condition): adds the Penalty Options of **Chimeric Mutation** (Aberration, Demonic, Monstrosity) and **Transmogrified Biology** (Sage Beast), word for word; the summary and note list every designation.
2. **Tidal Draw** (Ninjutsu Specialist, Tsunami): a new condition card (Class & Clan Conditions › Ninjutsu Specialist). Surging Maelstrom and Surging Maelstrom: Abyss get a **Tidal Draw** button (targets; until the start of your next turn). The temporary chakra is rolled at the table.
3. **Chakra Barrier** (Scout-Nin, Barrier Scout): the book's "Unique Amp" box is an Amp card, typeless with the note "Unique Amp", in three versions (Mastery on Chakra Control: 1, 2 at 9th, 3 at 17th). The feature's **Create Chakra Barrier** button starts it (after a rest); its hit points stay the Chakra Barrier pool; at 0 the Amp is ended on the Amps panel. The feature's own Mastery effect is gone (the Amp carries it).
4. **Primal Chain** (Weapon Specialist, Primal Weapon): its box is now also on Primal Strikes, the feature that grants it (Cross Synergy).

## Open

- **Summoner's Bonded creature** (Ninjutsu Specialist): no stat block in the book (its Design Note: not final). Needs King's rules.
- Built on a reading: Chakra Barrier is typeless (no Unique Amp type exists); it counts toward Rule of 3's like other typeless Amps.
